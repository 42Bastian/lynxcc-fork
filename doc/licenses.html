1. Summary

lynxcc is the Lynx Game Development SDK: a complete toolkit for building Atari Lynx games. Its toolchain — the C compiler, macro assembler, linker, librarian and related utilities — is derived from cc65 and forms one component of the SDK. The SDK also ships the runtime and the optional subsystem libraries, and will grow to include additional tools and libraries over time. The whole package is distributed under the cc65 license, a zlib-style permissive license reproduced in section 2.

The cc65-derived components carry the cc65 copyright (Copyright © 1998–2012 Ullrich von Bassewitz and contributors). Components added by this project, and any tools and libraries added to the SDK later, are distributed under the same zlib-style terms unless their own notice on this page states otherwise.

Two further notices apply to specific parts and are reproduced in full below: the original Atari 8-bit compiler copyright by John R. Dunning (section 3), which still covers small parts of the C preprocessor and main parser, and the notices for the bundled decompression components (section 4).

This page is the canonical registry for license and copyright notices in the SDK documentation; as new tools and libraries are added, their notices are recorded here. The same notices also appear in the top-level LICENSE file and in the headers of the individual source files they cover; consolidating them here does not remove them from any source file, and the per-file notices remain in place as required by the license.
